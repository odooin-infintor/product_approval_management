Odoo Product Module

Product Approval Management for Odoo

Put a proper review step in front of every product before it can be sold, bought, invoiced, or moved. Our Product Approval Management module adds a clear Draft → Under Approval → Approved workflow to the product form. Only designated Product Managers can approve or reject, rejections require a reason, and unapproved products are automatically blocked from Sales, Purchase, Invoicing and Inventory documents.

Why it works

Key Features of Product Approval Management

Automatic Approval Submission

Every new product is routed to Under Approval the moment it's saved – no separate "send for approval" step to remember, and no product slips through as an untracked Draft.

Server-Side Protected Approvals

Approve and Reject are guarded on the server, not just hidden in the UI. Even a call from developer mode outside the Product Manager group is refused, with a clear error message.

Configurable Approval Group

A dedicated Product Manager access group decides who can approve. Assign it to exactly the people responsible for sign-off from Settings → Users, in a couple of clicks.

Mass Approve & Mass Reject

Clear a backlog of pending products in two clicks. Select several from the list view and approve them together, or reject them all with one shared reason.

Role-Based Approve & Reject

Only Product Managers ever see the Approve, Reject and Reset to Draft buttons. Everyone else keeps creating and editing products exactly as before.

Mandatory Reject Reasons

Rejecting opens a wizard that won't confirm without a reason. That reason is then pinned to the product as a persistent red banner, so nobody has to ask why.

Chatter & Notification Alerts

Submission, approval and rejection are all logged to the chatter and pushed as a notification – to every Product Manager on submission, and to the creator on the outcome.

Enforced Everywhere

Unapproved products simply don't appear in Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, Invoices, Vendor Bills, or Inventory transfers – including receipts, deliveries, returns and scraps.

How it works

Screenshots Walkthrough

A complete walk-through of the approval workflow, from configuring the Product Manager role to seeing unapproved products blocked across Purchase, Invoicing and Inventory.

1

Configure the Product Manager Role

From Settings → Users, set the new Product Approval field to Product Manager for anyone who should be able to approve or reject products.

2

Create a New Product

Any new product starts life in Draft. As soon as it's saved, it's automatically submitted and moves to Under Approval.

3

Under Approval Products List

Product Managers open the dedicated Under Approval Products menu to see every product waiting on a decision, in one filtered list.

4

Review & Approve a Product

Opening a product under review shows the Approve, Reject and Reset to Draft buttons, plus a full chatter history of every status change.

5

Reject with a Required Reason

Clicking Reject opens a wizard that won't confirm until a reason is entered, keeping every rejection accountable.

6

Mass Approve & Mass Reject

Select several products from the list view and use the Actions menu to Mass Approve or Mass Not Approve them all at once.

7

Mass Reject Wizard

Mass Not Approve opens the same wizard listing every selected product, applying one shared reject reason to all of them.

8
Blocked

Blocked on Purchase Orders

An unapproved product can't be selected on a Request for Quotation – it simply doesn't appear as an existing product to pick.

9
Blocked

Blocked on Customer Invoices

The same protection applies to invoicing – an unapproved product can't be added to a Customer Invoice line either.

10
Blocked

Blocked on Inventory Transfers

Trying to validate a receipt containing an unapproved product raises a clear Validation Error: only Approved products can be moved in Inventory.
